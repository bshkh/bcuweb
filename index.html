FAQ

Common questions about ordering uniforms in the UAE

What is your minimum order quantity?

The standard minimum depends on the garment and production run. Confirm the current minimum with the team when requesting a quote.

How long does it take to make custom uniforms in Dubai?

Timing depends on the garment, sample approval, quantity and specification. The quotation should state the committed delivery date.

Do you deliver outside Dubai?

Yes. The specification covers delivery to Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.

Can I see a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. The approved sample becomes the quality reference for the bulk run.

Do you provide the fabric?

Yes. Common uniform fabrics include poly cotton blends, twill, gabardine, oxford, pique and flame resistant fabric.

Embroidery or printing?

Embroidery is suited to durable corporate and hospitality branding. Printing is useful for larger, detailed or full-colour artwork and sportswear.

What fabric works best in UAE heat?

For outdoor roles, lightweight poly cotton blends in the 150 to 180 GSM range can balance breathability and durability. The right choice depends on the role.

Do you do on-site measurement?

Yes. The team can visit your premises for qualifying orders and create a reusable sizing record.
