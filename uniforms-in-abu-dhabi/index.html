7.2 Uniforms in Abu Dhabi

Subhead: Specification led uniform supply for Abu Dhabi’s government, energy, healthcare and hospitality sectors, with the documentation their procurement processes require.

Intro: Abu Dhabi buys uniforms differently from Dubai. Government entities, energy operators and large institutions dominate demand, and they buy through formal procurement with written specifications, documentation requirements and contractual delivery dates. We manufacture in Bur Dubai and deliver to Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and the Western Region, working to written specification with documented quality control.

Industries we supply most in Abu Dhabi

Government and semi government. Formal service uniforms, municipal and inspection wear, reception and administrative uniforms, supplied against written specification with full documentation.

Oil, gas and energy. Flame resistant coveralls and workwear for operators and contractors across Musaffah, Ruwais and the offshore support base.

Healthcare. Scrubs, lab coats and clinical wear for the emirate’s hospitals and clinics, including department colour coding programmes.

Hospitality. Hotel and resort uniforms across the Corniche, Yas Island, Saadiyat Island and Al Ain properties.

Construction and infrastructure. High visibility site workwear for the emirate’s ongoing infrastructure and development programmes.

Education. School uniform programmes for private and public schools across Abu Dhabi city and Al Ain.

Facility management. Cleaning, maintenance and technical staff uniforms for the FM companies servicing Abu Dhabi’s commercial and residential portfolio.

Delivery and measurement in Abu Dhabi

Why Abu Dhabi organisations work with us

We work to a written specification. We quote line by line against your document rather than proposing our own substitutes, and we flag any item where market availability differs before quoting.

We supply the documentation procurement needs. Licence, VAT, fabric specifications, certificates where applicable, sample approval records and quality control records.

We manufacture, so the date is ours. A trading company’s delivery date depends on a shipment arriving. Ours depends on our own production floor.

We batch measurement visits efficiently. For Al Ain and Western Region clients we schedule measurement across multiple sites in one trip, which keeps the service free rather than chargeable.

Abu Dhabi specific FAQs

Do you deliver uniforms to Abu Dhabi? Yes, to Abu Dhabi city, Al Ain and the Western Region including Ruwais and Madinat Zayed. .

Can you supply against an Abu Dhabi government tender? Yes. We hold a valid Dubai trade licence and VAT registration and can supply the standard documentation set. Send us the tender documents and we will confirm within working days whether we can meet the specification and deadline.

Do you supply flame resistant workwear for Abu Dhabi oil and gas operations? Yes. We produce FR garments from certified FR fabric. Tell us the standard your operator requires and we will quote against it exactly, and tell you honestly whether certification sits at fabric level or garment level.

Will you visit Abu Dhabi for measurement? Yes, free for orders above pieces. For Al Ain and the Western Region we batch visits across sites to keep the service efficient.

How long does delivery to Abu Dhabi take compared to Dubai? Production time is identical. Delivery adds roughly additional day compared with Dubai.

Do you supply uniforms to Abu Dhabi free zones and industrial areas? Yes, including Musaffah, ICAD, KIZAD and the Ruwais industrial area.
