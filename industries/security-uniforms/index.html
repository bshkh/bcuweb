6.5 Security Uniforms

Subhead: Structured, hard wearing security uniforms built for long outdoor shifts in UAE conditions, with rank markings, reflective options and fast replacement.

Intro: A security uniform has to do two jobs at once. It has to project authority, which means structure, a clean press and a formal cut. And it has to be wearable for a twelve hour shift outdoors in forty five degree heat. Most suppliers optimise for one and sacrifice the other. We specify around both, and we produce in Bur Dubai so replacement garments reach your sites quickly.

The heat problem, and how to specify around it

The single biggest failure in UAE security uniforms is specifying a heavy, formal fabric because it looks authoritative, and then discovering that guards on outdoor posts are physically struggling with it by mid morning.

Our approach: split the specification by post type.

Structure where it is visible, breathability where it is not, is the principle. Fused collars and shoulder construction give the formal look. Fabric weight and back panel construction handle the heat.

Identification, rank and compliance

Security uniforms in Dubai and across the UAE carry identification requirements set by the relevant regulatory authority, and guarding companies operating in Dubai will be familiar with the SIRA framework. We produce to the identification requirements you provide, including company badge, licence identification, rank markings and any specified colour restrictions.

Important note for the build team: do not state or imply that the company is approved, certified or licensed by any regulator unless the client holds documentation proving it. Write to what the client can evidence. Publishing an unverified compliance claim on a security page is a material risk.

FAQs

What fabric is best for security uniforms in UAE summer? For outdoor posts, a lightweight poly cotton blend in the 140 to 160 GSM range with a breathable back yoke. It holds a press, which matters for appearance, while staying wearable through a long shift. Heavier fabric looks smarter in a photograph and performs badly in August.

Can you supply epaulettes, rank slides and company badges? Yes, all produced in house and applied to the garment, so you receive a complete uniform rather than parts to assemble.

How fast can you supply uniforms for new guards? Once we hold your pattern, fabric and badge file, a single set typically takes working days. That is the main practical reason guarding companies move from a trading supplier to a manufacturer.

Do you supply female security officer uniforms? Yes, cut to a female fit rather than a smaller version of the male pattern, with modest wear options including longer length tunics and hijab options in matching fabric.

What is your minimum order? pieces per design for a new programme. Reorders against an existing pattern have no practical minimum.

Can you produce uniforms to our client’s specified colours for a specific contract? Yes. Contract specific colour variants are common in guarding, and we hold each variant as a separate reference so there is no cross contamination between contracts.
