The fabric decision for school uniforms

School uniforms face a harder test than almost any other category. A shirt is washed twice a week for forty weeks, worn by a child who does not treat it carefully, and exposed to strong sun during break. The failure modes are always the same: colour fades, collars go grey, and seams split at the shoulder.

Our recommendations for UAE schools:

We reinforce the shoulder seams and the trouser seat on every school garment as standard, because those are the two points that fail first.

House colours and crests

School crests are usually detailed, often with fine text and multiple colours, which makes them one of the harder embroidery jobs. We digitise the crest once, produce a stitch out on your actual shirt fabric for the school to approve, and store the file permanently. That means the crest on a shirt bought in 2030 matches the crest on a shirt bought today, which matters when siblings are wearing them side by side.

For house colours we hold or source fabric in the specific shades the school uses, and record the reference so the yellow house shirt is the same yellow every year.

How much does a school uniform cost in Dubai? It depends on the garment and the quantity. As a guide, a poly cotton school shirt at volume sits around AED to AED per piece, trousers around AED to AED , and a blazer considerably more. Crest embroidery adds roughly AED per garment. We quote per garment at your exact quantity rather than giving a per student figure, because schools differ widely in how many pieces each student needs.

How far in advance should a school order for a new academic year? Place the order by for an term start. That allows time for sample approval, a fitting day, production and delivery without compressing the quality check. Schools that come to us in can usually still be served, but the options narrow.

Can you match the uniform our current supplier makes? Usually yes. Send us a sample garment and we will match the fabric weight, colour and cut. Where an exact shade match is not possible on a new fabric batch, we tell you before you commit rather than after delivery.

Do you supply modest wear options? Yes. We produce school abayas, hijabs in matching colours, long sleeve and longer length options, and full length PE wear in the same fabrics as the standard kit.

Can parents order directly from you? Yes, where the school agrees to that arrangement. We can set up a simple ordering and collection process, or deliver to the school for distribution.

Do you provide PE kit and sports team uniforms as well? Yes. PE kit is part of most school programmes we supply, and we also produce sports team kits with sublimated names and numbers for inter school competition.
