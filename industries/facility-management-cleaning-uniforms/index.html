Dignity in the specification

A point worth stating directly to FM buyers: the quality of a cleaning uniform affects how the staff wearing it are treated by the public. A thin, poorly fitted, quickly faded uniform signals something to everyone who passes, and it affects retention.

Specifying one weight up, choosing a colour that does not grey quickly, and cutting to a proper fit rather than issuing three sizes costs a small amount more per piece. FM companies that do it report measurably better retention, and retention is the largest controllable cost in this sector.

Multi contract colour variants

Most FM companies run several client contracts, and clients often want their site teams in a specific colour or with the client’s logo. We hold each contract as a separate specification under your account, with its own colour reference, logo file and size list, so a reorder for Contract A never accidentally arrives in Contract B’s colours.

Fabric for cleaning environments

Cleaning uniforms meet chemicals daily. Bleach, degreasers and disinfectants attack colour aggressively.

We recommend a poly cotton blend at 180 to 210 GSM with a high polyester content for colour retention, in colours selected for chemical resistance. Deep navy, charcoal, black and strong mid blues hold up well. Light pastels, bright reds and anything in the orange family fade quickly under chemical exposure and should be used only on trim and accents rather than on the main body panel.

FAQs

Can you supply different uniform colours for different client contracts? Yes. Each contract is held as a separate specification with its own colour, logo and size list, so reorders stay correct.

What fabric survives cleaning chemicals best? A poly cotton blend at 180 to 210 GSM with a high polyester content, in a deep colour. Bleach and disinfectant strip colour from cotton rich and light coloured fabrics quickly.

How quickly can you supply uniforms for new staff? Once your patterns and logo are on file, typically working days for a small quantity. FM turnover makes this the most important factor in supplier choice, which is why we hold everything on record.

Do you supply uniforms for maintenance and technical staff too? Yes, including MEP technician uniforms, electrician workwear, coveralls, and landscaping teams.

Can you put our client’s logo on uniforms rather than ours? Yes, with written confirmation from you that permission has been granted.
