6.15 Logistics, Warehouse And Delivery Uniforms

Subhead: Branded driver, rider and warehouse uniforms designed for constant movement and UAE heat, with visibility options and volume pricing.

Intro: Delivery and logistics work is physical, outdoors in part, and relentless. A rider in July and a warehouse picker in a non air conditioned facility are both operating in heat while moving constantly. The uniform has to be visible, breathable and brand consistent across a large and changing workforce. We supply logistics, courier, last mile and warehousing companies across the UAE.

Specifying for movement and heat

Moisture management is the priority. Polyester pique or micro mesh at 140 to 170 GSM moves sweat away and dries fast. Cotton holds sweat, becomes heavy, and stays wet.

Visibility for last mile riders. Riders share roads with traffic. Reflective elements on the back, shoulders and sleeves are worth specifying even where they are not mandated.

Freedom of movement. Set in sleeves restrict overhead reach. Raglan sleeves or a gusseted underarm make a measurable difference in warehouse picking roles.

Colour and heat. Dark branded colours absorb heat badly on outdoor rider roles. Where brand colour is dark, use it on the collar, sleeve and back panel and keep the body lighter.

Managing a large, changing workforce

Delivery and logistics run high turnover. The practical requirement is not a beautiful uniform, it is a uniform that can be issued to a new rider on their first day, every day, without a procurement cycle.

We handle this by holding your pattern, logo and size grid, producing to a simplified size range that covers the workforce without needing individual measurement, and running scheduled top up batches rather than one large annual order.

FAQs

What fabric is best for delivery riders in UAE summer? Polyester pique or micro mesh at 140 to 170 GSM. It wicks sweat and dries fast. Cotton feels better for the first hour and worse for the next eight.

Can you add reflective elements to branded rider polos? Yes, as heat sealed reflective tape or reflective print on the back, shoulders and sleeves.

Can you supply uniforms on a regular top up schedule? Yes, and we recommend it for logistics. We agree a monthly or quarterly batch based on your hiring rate, so new starters are always covered.

Do you supply warehouse uniforms as well as driver uniforms? Yes, and the specification differs. Warehouse roles need more movement in the shoulder and a heavier fabric for durability. Driver roles need lighter fabric and visibility.

Can you brand uniforms for a fleet operating under a client’s name? Yes, with written confirmation that you hold permission to use that branding.
