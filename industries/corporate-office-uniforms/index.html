Getting the brand right

A corporate uniform fails when it looks like a promotional giveaway. Three things separate a corporate programme that staff wear willingly from one they avoid.

Fit. A well cut shirt in a mid range fabric beats a poorly cut shirt in an expensive one. We produce in slim, regular and relaxed fits, with a separate female cut rather than a smaller male pattern.

Logo restraint. One embroidered logo on the left chest, in thread colours matched to the brand, reads as professional. The same logo printed large across the back reads as merchandise. We advise on placement, and for most corporate clients the answer is smaller than they initially ask for.

Colour discipline. Match to your brand Pantone and confirm on a physical swatch. A near miss on a brand colour is more noticeable on a shirt than anywhere else, because it sits next to your business cards and your signage.

FAQs

What is the minimum order for company polo shirts in Dubai? pieces. Corporate polos run on stock fabric, which is why the minimum is lower here than for fully tailored garments.

How much does a branded corporate polo shirt cost? At volume, a good quality 220 GSM pique polo with a single embroidered chest logo typically sits around AED to AED per piece. Price varies with fabric weight, quantity and the number of logo positions. We quote at your exact quantity.

Should we use embroidery or printing for our company logo? Embroidery for shirts, polos, blazers and caps, because it lasts longer and reads as more professional. Printing for large designs, full colour artwork, or event t shirts where cost per piece matters more than longevity.

Can you match our exact brand colour? We match to a Pantone reference and confirm on a physical fabric swatch before production. Fabric takes dye differently from paper, so we always confirm on the actual material rather than on screen.

Can you supply different garments for different departments? Yes. A common structure is tailored wear for client facing roles, polos for operations, and branded t shirts for events, all sharing the same logo treatment and colour palette so the brand stays coherent.

Do you supply uniforms for exhibitions and events? Yes, including rapid turnaround for event dates. Tell us the date at enquiry stage and we will confirm honestly whether it is achievable.
