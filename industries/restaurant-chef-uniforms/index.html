6.7 Restaurant, Cafe And Chef Uniforms

Subhead: Kitchen and front of house uniforms built for heat, stains and a fast reorder cycle, supplied to restaurants, cafes and cloud kitchens across the UAE.

Intro: Restaurant uniforms fail faster than almost any other category. Kitchen whites stain, aprons fray, and front of house shirts get through a wash cycle far more often than office wear. We supply restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens and catering companies across the UAE, and we design the programme around replacement cost rather than only around how it looks on day one.

The kitchen safety point most suppliers skip

Polyester melts. In a kitchen environment with open flame, hot oil and hot surfaces, a high polyester chef jacket is a burn injury risk, because melted synthetic fabric adheres to skin in a way that cotton does not.

We specify cotton rich fabric for chef jackets and trousers as standard, typically a 200 to 240 GSM cotton or cotton rich blend. It is heavier and creases more than polyester. That trade off is the correct one, and any supplier offering a cheap, light, fully synthetic chef jacket is optimising for their margin rather than your team.

Double breasted construction exists for the same reason: the front panel can be reversed to hide a spill mid service, and the double layer gives some protection across the chest.

Stain, colour and replacement planning

White chef jackets are traditional and they stain. That is a fact of the category and no fabric finish fully solves it. The practical answers are a stain release finish, which helps oil based marks lift in the wash, and colour choice for roles where white is not required, with black and charcoal being the standard alternatives.

Front of house is where colour choice does most of the work. Mid tone colours show wear fastest. Dark colours and deep tones hold their appearance far longer through frequent washing. For a 30 seat cafe replacing shirts twice a year, that difference is real money.

FAQs

Where can I buy chef coats in Dubai in bulk? We manufacture them in Bur Dubai and supply across the UAE. Minimum order is pieces per design, with reorders against an existing pattern available in smaller quantities.

Should chef jackets be cotton or polyester? Cotton or cotton rich. Polyester near open flame and hot oil is a burn risk because it melts. We specify cotton rich fabric in the 200 to 240 GSM range for chef jackets as standard and we do not recommend high polyester kitchen wear regardless of the price advantage.

Do you supply aprons with our logo? Yes, in canvas, denim, poly cotton and waxed finishes, with embroidered or printed logos. Cross back barista aprons and adjustable strap styles are both standard.

How do I stop chef whites from staining? Specify a stain release finish, wash promptly rather than letting oil set, and avoid chlorine bleach, which yellows cotton over time. Where white is not a requirement, black or charcoal jackets dramatically reduce the replacement cycle.

Can you supply uniforms for a new restaurant opening? Yes. Tell us your opening date at enquiry and we will confirm honestly whether it is achievable, and phase delivery if your team is onboarding in stages.

What is the cheapest way to uniform a small cafe? A branded polo shirt with a single embroidered chest logo plus a good quality apron. That combination costs a fraction of tailored service wear and looks deliberate rather than cheap, provided the polo is a 200 GSM or better fabric.
