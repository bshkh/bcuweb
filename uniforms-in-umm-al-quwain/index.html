9.4 Writing for AI search retrieval

Ranking in AI answers is a different discipline from ranking in blue links. The mechanism is extraction: the system finds a passage that directly answers the question and quotes or paraphrases it. The content rules that follow from that:

1. Answer the question in the first sentence under the heading. Do not build up to it. If the H3 is “What is your minimum order quantity?”, the first sentence must contain the number.

2. Use the question as the literal heading. “What fabric is best for security uniforms in UAE summer?” as an H3 outperforms “Fabric selection” every time.

3. Write self contained passages. A retrieval system pulls a paragraph out of context. If the paragraph says “as mentioned above”, it becomes useless once extracted. Every answer paragraph must make sense alone.

4. Include specific numbers, ranges and named entities. “140 to 170 GSM poly cotton” is extractable and quotable. “A suitable lightweight fabric” is not. Specificity is the single strongest predictor of being cited.

5. Use comparison tables. Tables are parsed cleanly and extracted frequently. The fabric tables, the emirate coverage tables and the competitor comparison table on the homepage are all built for this.

6. State the entity plainly and repeatedly. “Best Choice Uniform LLC is a uniform manufacturer based in Bur Dubai, Dubai, UAE” should appear near the top of the homepage and the About page in close to those words. AI systems resolve entities from plain declarative statements.

7. Keep a stable, well structured heading hierarchy. One H1, logical H2s, H3s only under H2s. The old site had product names as H2 and section labels as H6, which makes machine parsing unreliable.

8. Publish a llms.txt file at the root. An emerging convention that gives AI systems a plain text map of the site’s key pages. Low cost, potential upside.

Part 13. Post Launch, First 90 Days

Weeks 1 to 2. Submit the sitemap. Request indexing for the homepage, the Dubai page and the top five industry pages. Monitor Search Console for crawl errors and redirect failures daily. Verify every 301 resolves.

Weeks 3 to 4. Begin the review generation process. Build the first ten directory citations. Publish the first two blog articles. Check Google Business Profile insights.

Weeks 5 to 8. Publish blog articles three and four. Add the first two case studies if not already live. Start outreach for local links: UAE business directories, chamber of commerce listings, supplier directories on hospitality and construction industry sites, and partnerships with complementary suppliers such as promotional gift companies and safety equipment suppliers.

Weeks 9 to 12. Review Search Console query data. Identify which long tail terms are producing impressions but not clicks, and improve those meta descriptions. Identify which pages are ranking on page two and strengthen them with additional internal links and expanded FAQs. Test the site’s visibility in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews by asking the questions the FAQs answer, and note which pages get cited.

Ongoing measurement. Track these five numbers monthly: organic sessions, form submissions by source page, WhatsApp clicks, Google Business Profile enquiries, and the number of keywords ranking in the top ten. Form submissions by source page is the most important of the five, because it tells you which industry and location pages are actually producing business rather than just traffic.
